        How it works

        1. Choose what to convert

          Paste a link to a video or a whole playlist, or pick an audio or video file from your computer.

        2. Pick a format

          Choose audio or video, then a format and quality. The details panel shows what you will get.

        3. Download the result

          Links are fetched with yt-dlp and converted with FFmpeg. Temporary files are deleted when the download finishes.

        Formats

        Not sure which one to pick? MP3 for music on any device, MP4 for video that plays everywhere.

        Supported output formats
        FormatTypeGood for
        MP3AudioMusic and podcasts on almost any device or player.
        M4AAudioBetter quality than MP3 at the same size. Works on Apple devices and most phones.
        AACAudioA raw AAC stream. Fewer players support it than M4A.
        WAVAudioUncompressed sound for editing. Files are large.
        FLACAudioLossless sound at roughly half the size of WAV.
        OGGAudioOpen format used by games and many Linux players.
        OpusAudioEfficient at low bitrates, so it suits speech.
        MP4VideoThe safest choice. Plays on phones, TVs and browsers.
        WebMVideoOpen format made for browsers. Conversion is slower.
        MKVVideoFlexible container for archiving. Some TVs cannot play it.

        Questions

        Why does it say FFmpeg was not found?

        FFmpeg does the merging and converting, and it has to be installed on your computer. The README explains how to install it on Windows, macOS and Linux, and how to point MediaForge at it if it is installed but not detected.

        Which sites can I paste links from?

        Any site that yt-dlp supports. YouTube links also need a JavaScript runtime, which the setup installs for you.

        Are my files uploaded anywhere?

        No. Conversion happens on your own computer. Links are downloaded straight from the source site, and files you choose never leave your machine.

        Why is there a length limit?

        Videos longer than 60 minutes and uploads over 500 MB are refused by default, so one request cannot fill your disk. Both limits can be changed in the settings described in the README.

        What am I allowed to download?

        Use MediaForge for content you own or have permission to copy, such as your own uploads or Creative Commons work. Many sites restrict downloading in their terms, and copyright law applies.

        Settings

        Sites that need you signed in

        YouTube sometimes asks to confirm you are not a bot, and sites such as Reddit hide some videos from visitors. MediaForge can use the sign-in from a browser on this computer. It stays on this computer.

        Firefox works most reliably. With Chrome or Edge, close the browser first, as recent versions can block this. A spare account is safer, because sites may limit accounts that download a lot.

        Speed

        Two is a good start. Choose 1 if YouTube keeps asking for a sign-in check.

        Connection

        Sends requests through a proxy you control or trust, such as one from your own VPN app. Leave it empty to connect directly. A proxy can see the sites you visit.

        Keep downloads working

        Sites change often, and yt-dlp is the part that keeps up with them.
