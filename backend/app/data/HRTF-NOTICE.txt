hrtf.sofa is the "MIT KEMAR, normal pinna" head-related transfer function set, in SOFA format
(the same file that ships with the free libmysofa library).

Measurements: Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, MIT Media Lab, 1994,
"HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone". The MIT Media Lab allows free use of the
data, including in commercial work, when the source is credited.

MediaForge only uses it to make FFmpeg's "sofalizer" filter place sound around the head.
